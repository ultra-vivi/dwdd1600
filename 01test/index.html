Digital Citizenship

What us Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship means using the internet safely, responsibly, and respectfully.

What is Digital Law?

Digital law refers to the rules and legal principles that govern behavior, information, and activities online.

What is AUP?

AUP 1s a formal set of guidelines that defines what users can and cannot do when using a network, website, or digital system.

What is Plagarism?

Plagiarism is using someone else's words, ideas, or work and pretending it's your own, without giving proper credit.

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Any behavior in an academic setting that violates honesty, fairness, and integrity.

What are the rules for AI Generated Content?

Rules for using Ai Generated Content include madatory disclosure, human creativity, no deceptive or manipulated media, and must comply to copyright and monetization rules.
