Who Were the Signers

Famous Signers

Several signers of the Declaration of Independence stand out as especially influential because of their later roles in shaping the United States. Their political leadership, public visibility, and contributions to the Revolution made them some of the most widely recognized figures among the 56 delegates who signed the document.

John Hancock
President of the Second Continental Congress, remembered for his large and iconic signature on the Declaration.
Thomas Jefferson
Principal author of the Declaration of Independence and later the third President of the United States.
Benjamin Franklin
Renowned diplomat, inventor, writer, and scientist who helped secure French support during the Revolution.
John Adams
Key advocate for independence, member of the drafting committee, and later the second President of the United States.
Samuel Adams
Influential patriot leader known for organizing resistance efforts such as the Boston Tea Party.
Charles Carroll of Carrollton
One of the wealthiest men in the colonies and the last surviving signer, living until 1832.

These signers stand out because several became pesidents or major statesmen, were well-known and have a historical legacy.
